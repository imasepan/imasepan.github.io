It's been a while since I wrote here. So much has happened. But I want to talk about today.

October 1st is a rainy day.
And I didn't bring an umbrella. 

Today I decided to skip class - my friends asked me to get lunch (we went to a KBBQ place - which was pretty good despite it being centred in Lafayette) and I had gone to bed at 2AM in the morning after taking a particularly nasty Electrical Engineering exam. 

Not to digress but it sucks when you spend so much time studying for this exam, reviewing the homeworks, the available past exams, and online lectures, only for the content on the exam to focus on extremely niche concepts and have many questions on content mostly divorced from what appears on what study material I could gather. It feels like all of my efforts were for nought. Pretty depressing - but hopefully the next 3 exams are better.

So far the semester has been fruitful emotionally. I spend most of my time with my friends, studying or completing assignments. I try to finish my work by Thursday, though lately I must be slowing down in pace as I have two things due by this Friday. Gotta lock in.
